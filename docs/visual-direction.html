Direction notes

05 / rules, not preferences

Do

Let fog bleed full width. Keep body copy under 52ch. Use cyan only for live state. Label illustrations. Make every screen work as a still.

Don't

No neon-on-black gamer glow, no fake countdowns, no stock handshakes, no text parallax, no glass where a hairline would do.

Imagery

Frost on a coil, condensation on copper, a plant room at blue hour. Cool grade, desaturated, real wear. No smiles to camera.

Sound

None. The chamber is silent. Motion carries the temperature.
